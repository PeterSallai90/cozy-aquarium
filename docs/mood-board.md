# Mood board

Mood and polish only. Where this board and docs/art-style.md differ, docs/art-style.md wins.

- Chosen direction: **Dream Glow**, a softly glowing, gently saturated tank where the lamp, the
  fish and the decor carry the colour, with a painterly, dreamy bloom while plants, stone and water
  stay natural. The owner picked it as a single candidate, with no mix:
  [choice logged on #61](https://github.com/PeterSallai90/cozy-aquarium/issues/61#issuecomment-6672290114).
- This board is a reference, not a review gate. Art changes are compared against the golden scene
  (#62) once it exists.
- It holds no rule, value or hex code. Rules live in [docs/art-style.md](art-style.md). Changing a
  rule to match the board is a separate issue, and a change to D5 needs a new ADR.
- Links only: no images are stored in the repository, and no entry asks to reproduce a specific
  game's look.
- Legend: the source kinds are Stylized game, Aquascaping, Lighting or underwater photography and
  Concept art. "Main" is freshwater home-aquarium material. "Secondary mood only" says why an entry
  is not Main, for example a game scene or open ocean.
- Refinement of the direction later goes through a separate issue.

## Palette

Colour words only, as impressions of the references. They stay inside the tint ranges of
[docs/art-style.md](art-style.md).

- rose-gold lamp glow: the key light over the tank and its soft bloom
- soft teal light: a subtle glow in the water, never a heavy tint
- tetra stripe blue: fish colour
- coral red: fish and coloured decor
- dusk violet: the dim room around the tank
- natural moss green: plants, varying from yellow-green to deep blue-green
- slate blue-grey: rocks and stone
- warm brown: wood
- warm sand: cream to beige substrate
